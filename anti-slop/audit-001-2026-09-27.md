## Remediation & Resolution Report

All 8 approved findings have been remediated across the codebase:

1. **Finding 001 (R-04 Emoji in Portal):** Replaced raw Unicode characters and emoji (`✕`, `⚠`, `📋`, `✓`, `⚡`) in `CandidatePortal.tsx` with semantic Phosphor icon components (`XCircle`, `WarningCircle`, `FileText`, `CheckCircle`).
2. **Finding 002 (R-04 Emoji in Demo):** Replaced literal symbols in `DemoSimulator.tsx` sample preset buttons (`✓`, `⚠`, `✕`) with clear text labels (`Strong Senior Answer`, `Basic / Brief Answer`, `Evasive Answer`).
3. **Finding 003 (R-01/13 Radial Glow Background):** Removed decorative full-page blurred glow orbs (`blur-3xl`) from `CandidatePortal.tsx`.
4. **Finding 004 (R-08 Residual Button Arrows):** Removed trailing arrows (`→`, `←`) from action buttons and link pills in `Jobs.tsx`, `Careers.tsx`, `Dashboard.tsx`, `InterviewResult.tsx`, `CVs.tsx`, and `Candidates.tsx`.
5. **Finding 005 (R-15 Generic CTAs):** Replaced "Get Started Free" / "Get Started" in `PublicLayout.tsx` with explicit "Create Recruiter Account" and "Create Account".
6. **Finding 006 (R-04 Generic AI Icons):** Replaced generic `Lightning` and `Star` icons in `Dashboard.tsx`, `Landing.tsx`, `CompanyContext.tsx`, and `Careers.tsx` with content-relevant icons (`ChatCircleText`, `Copy`, `Brain`, `CheckCircle`).
7. **Finding 007 (R-19 Endless Bounce Loop):** Removed `animate-bounce` perpetual animation from warning icon in `TimerGate.tsx`.
8. **Finding 008 (R-27/38 Inconsistent Email Fallback):** Normalized empty candidate email displays in `Candidates.tsx` and `Candidate360Drawer.tsx` to consistent muted `"-"`.
